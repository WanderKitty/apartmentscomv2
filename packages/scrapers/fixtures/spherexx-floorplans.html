Downtown Orlando's Height of Luxury

At 55 West, our residents can find all that they need for a life of luxury and ease. Pet Friendly Features

55 West Apartments is a pet-friendly community designed to make city living comfortable for both residents and their furry companions. The property features an on-site pet park where dogs can run and play, while nearby pet services—including veterinary care, grooming, and pet supply stores—add extra convenience for pet owners. With spacious apartment layouts and a welcoming pet policy that allows up to two pets per home, 55 West makes it easy to enjoy downtown Orlando living with your four-legged family members.

Perks of Our
Neighborhood

Imagine living in the heart of Downtown Orlando, surrounded by the best dining, shopping, and entertainment the city has to offer. Just steps away from Lake Eola Park and Lake Cherokee Park, our luxury apartments at 55 West provide a vibrant lifestyle for those who appreciate convenience and style. Enjoy dining at local favorites like 4 Rivers Smokehouse and Moe's Southwest Grill or take a short stroll to the vibrant Kia Center or the Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts. With a central downtown location, our residents can immerse themselves in the local arts and culture scene, making 55 West the ideal choice for urban explorers and culture enthusiasts.

Explore

Experience Orlando


For commuters, 55 West Apartments offers an unbeatable Downtown Orlando location with quick access to major roadways including I-4, SR 408 (East-West Expressway), and SR 417. Residents are just a short walk from the Church Street SunRail station and the LYNX Central transit hub, making it easy to connect throughout the Orlando metro area without relying on a car. Whether you're heading to the Central Business District, Orlando Health, or Orlando International Airport, 55 West puts you at the center of it all.
